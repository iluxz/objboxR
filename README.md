# objboxR

you found a cube. you didn't build it. you don't know where it came from. it was just there one day, spinning, watching.

this is the remastered version of the original objbox/cube. same lore, same obj, same void. rebuilt from scratch with three.js, better ui, better customization, and all the new songs from void records.

## what it is

- a gray outer cube that is semi-transparent because it can't fully contain whatever is inside
- 8 inner colored cubes that orbit in patterns that don't match any known math
- particles that are debris from whatever was here before you opened the tab
- a clock that shows void time (it happens to match your clock most of the time)
- an fps counter that measures dimensional coherence, not your gpu
- obj. obj has opinions. obj does not care about your feelings.
- jbo. obj's brother. lives in the reactor. screams.
- a terminal (cube#) with a fake filesystem, package manager, and scripting language
- a reactor you can actually melt down
- weather, seasons, weekly moods
- 100+ achievements
- a skill tree with 37 upgrades
- a lorebook with 17+ pages
- minigames: error page, obby, tower defense, mailroom, secret cube, casino, objpot, and more
- multiplayer via durable objects
- void records (114+ tracks) for music

## how to run

you don't. it's hosted.

- **github pages:** https://iluxz.github.io/objboxR/
- **local:** `python -m http.server 8080` then open `http://localhost:8080/cube.html`

## controls

- **mouse drag** — rotate
- **scroll** — zoom
- **1-5** — color themes
- **space** — toggle rotation
- **`** (backtick) — toggle terminal
- **F3** — reactor view
- **T** — reactor terminal

## terminal commands

type `help` in the terminal. there's a lot. cube, void, ping, transmit, ps, pwd, ls, cd, cat, mkdir, touch, echo, rm, cp, mv, tree, set, get, vars, gui, clear, math, display, screenshot, perfmode, uptime, visits, weather, travel, loop, sort, studio, exit, buymax, nothingcore, updates, bios, jedec, dream, mail, admin, eval, and more.

## obj

obj is the void's primary interface with intruders. obj responds to transmissions. obj has opinions. obj does not care about your feelings. obj's name was chosen by obj. you don't question it.

## jbo

jbo is obj's brother. lives in the reactor. hates nyarch. loud, chaotic, screaming in a nuclear facility. triggered via reactor terminal "nyarch". opposite of obj in every way but shares the same hatred.

## the lore

the cube was found, not built. you opened a new tab one day and it was there instead of your usual page. the gray outer cube is the void itself — a containment shell. the 8 inner cubes are fragments of something older. the particles are debris from whatever was here before you opened the tab.

drone2lp.wav isn't background music. it's the sound the void makes when it's watching you.

don't stare at the cube for too long. the inner cubes will start moving in patterns that match your breathing. that's not a coincidence.
